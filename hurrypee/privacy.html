Hurry Pee — Privacy Policy

Effective: April 2026 Last Updated: April 14, 2026

Hurry Pee (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application for iOS and watchOS.

1. Information We Collect

Hurry Pee does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The App only stores the following data locally on your device and your paired Apple Watch:

  • Reminder time slots (e.g., 08:00, 13:00)
  • Effective days (e.g., weekdays, weekends)
  • Reminder style (gentle, standard, silent)
  • Your “Done” or “Remind Later” completion records
  • Daily bowel movement logs (timestamp only)

2. Apple HealthKit Integration

With your explicit permission, Hurry Pee may read from and write to Apple Health:

  • Write: When you mark a “Done” event on Apple Watch, the App may write a bowel movement sample to Apple Health.
  • Read (future versions): To improve your regularity score, the App may read existing Health data.

You can enable or disable HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy → Health → Hurry Pee.

⚕️ All Health data stays on your device and iCloud account. We do not have access to your Health data.

3. Local Storage Only — No Cloud Servers

All your reminder settings and bowel logs are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple’s native storage (UserDefaults, CoreData, or similar).

We do not operate any external servers, databases, or analytics platforms.
We do not upload, sell, or share your data with any third party.

4. Watch Connectivity

Hurry Pee uses Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework to sync settings and records between your iPhone and Apple Watch.

This data is transmitted directly via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi between your devices and is never sent to us or any external server.

5. No Tracking, No Analytics, No Ads

We do not use:

  • Third-party analytics (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics)
  • Crash reporting services (unless Apple’s built-in crash logs, which are anonymous)
  • Advertising or tracking cookies
🚫 No trackers 📡 No servers 🛡️ Zero telemetry

6. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all your Hurry Pee data by:

  1. Uninstalling the App from your iPhone and Apple Watch, or
  2. Deleting the App’s data manually in iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Hurry Pee → Delete App

We do not have any remote deletion capability.

7. Children’s Privacy

Hurry Pee is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App or on this page, with an updated “Last Updated” date.

By using Hurry Pee, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
