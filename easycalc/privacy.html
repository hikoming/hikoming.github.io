Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 22, 2025

1. Introduction

EasyCalc ("the App") is a straightforward calculator application developed by MingMing Team. Your privacy is not an afterthought — it is built into the core design. This Privacy Policy explains exactly how we handle (or more importantly, how we do NOT handle) your information. By using EasyCalc, you agree to the terms described here.

✨ Zero data philosophy: No sign‑ups, no cloud sync, no analytics. Just a calculator that works entirely on your device.

2. No Data Collection

EasyCalc is built to respect your digital life from the ground up. We never collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • 🔒 No personal data — We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any identifying information.
  • 🔒 No accounts — The App requires no login, profile creation, or registration.
  • 🔒 100% local processing — Every calculation, every digit, every result stays on your device. No data is sent to any server or third‑party service.
  • 🔒 No cloud backup — We do not offer cloud storage of history or preferences, because that would require moving data outside your control. Everything remains offline.

In short: the only place your numbers ever exist is your own screen, and once you close the App, nothing is retained beyond what the operating system caches locally without any external transmission.

3. No Tracking or Analytics

We believe that a calculator does not need to spy on how you use it. Therefore:

  • 📊 No cookies — We do not use web cookies, local storage trackers, or any cross-session identifiers.
  • 📊 No analytics SDKs — EasyCalc contains no Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or any other usage tracking framework.
  • 📊 No behavioral monitoring — We never track button taps, session duration, crash logs (unless automatically handled by the OS with anonymized diagnostics that you control via system settings). Even then, we do not receive or associate data with individuals.
  • 📊 No third-party tracking libraries — The app is built without advertising or tracking software.

Your usage pattern is yours alone. We don’t know how often you calculate tips, solve equations, or simply open the app — because that information never reaches us.

4. Device Permissions

EasyCalc requests zero special permissions. The App does not require access to:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts or calendar
  • Location services (GPS)
  • Files or storage (unless strictly required by OS for basic app functionality, but EasyCalc does not read/write personal documents; any settings are local and sandboxed)
  • Bluetooth or NFC

The only “permission” EasyCalc relies on is the ability to run and display buttons — that is a standard system interaction. The app works completely offline and does not need internet connectivity.

💡 Offline by design: You can put your device in airplane mode, and EasyCalc functions exactly the same. No server handshake, no “phone home” behavior.

5. Children’s Privacy

EasyCalc is a simple utility tool that can be used by anyone, but it does not knowingly target children under the age of 13 (“Children”). We do not collect personal information from any user — regardless of age. Because we collect no data, there is no risk of inadvertently gathering data from children. Parents and guardians can rest assured that the app has no tracking, no social features, and no data sharing.

If you believe a child has provided any form of data to us (which is technically impossible given the app's design), please contact us, and we will ensure no residual concerns remain. However, the architecture of EasyCalc makes data collection infeasible.

6. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect clarifications or to meet evolving legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date at the top. Since the app collects no data, updates to the policy will never retroactively change how your information was handled — there is simply no data to mishandle. Your continued use of EasyCalc after any modification constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

We encourage you to review this document periodically. For significant changes, we may provide an in-app notice (e.g., when a major version updates) but you can always find the latest version here.

Compliance & Standards

EasyCalc’s privacy design aligns with major global frameworks and store requirements:

  • ✅ GDPR / CCPA ready — Explicit “no data collection” declaration means no consent pop-ups, no data subject requests needed because no data exists.
  • ✅ Apple App Store & Google Play — Covers mandatory sections: data use, tracking, permissions, and safety labels. The App qualifies for “Data Not Collected” status.
  • ✅ COPPA compliant — Because no personal information is collected from anyone, including children under 13.
  • ✅ Zero third-party sharing — We do not sell, trade, or transfer any information (there’s nothing to transfer).

This policy is transparent, written in plain English, and serves as a binding statement of our privacy promises.

⚙️ Technical assurance — EasyCalc does not include any network permission on Android (no INTERNET) and on iOS it does not use any networking APIs. The app is sandboxed and verified during development to contain zero data collection frameworks.
