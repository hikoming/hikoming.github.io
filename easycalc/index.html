⌚️ Apple Watch experience

EasyCalc is designed exclusively for watchOS and iPhone. All features are optimized for the small screen — large buttons, haptic feedback, and smart layout.

  • Complication support – Add EasyCalc to your watch face to launch the calculator instantly.
  • Standalone on Watch – Works without your phone nearby (Wi-Fi/cellular optional, but no internet required).
  • Tip & Split on wrist – Perfect for restaurant bills: calculate tip and split without pulling out your phone.
  • Discounts on the go – Scan a sale price, input percentage, and see what you’ll actually pay.
  • Historical sync via iCloud – (Optional) Your calculation history stays in sync across iPhone and Apple Watch using your private iCloud account; we never see that data.
💡 Pro tip: Use the “History” button on your watch to quickly reuse a previous calculation. Tap any past formula (e.g., “15% tip on $48.50”) to load it instantly.

📜 Historical Record & Smart Recall

EasyCalc keeps a local log of all calculations you perform, sorted from newest to oldest. It’s more than just a list — it actually helps you work faster.

  • Tap to reuse: In the history screen, tap any previous calculation (e.g., “23 × 4.5 = 103.5”). The formula loads directly into the main calculator, so you can edit or recalculate.
  • Clear history option: Delete individual records or wipe the entire history — all private and on-device.
  • No cloud shadow: History stays on your device unless you enable iCloud sync, which is end‑to‑end encrypted and never accessible by MingMing Team.
  • Works offline 100%: Even without internet, your history is always there.

This makes EasyCalc perfect for iterative budgeting, reusing complex discount chains, or just double-checking yesterday’s math.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does EasyCalc collect any personal data?
Absolutely none. EasyCalc does not collect names, emails, location, or usage stats. There are no third-party analytics, no crash reporting tools that identify you, and no ads. Your calculations remain 100% private on your device.
Is the Apple Watch version free? Does it have ads?
Yes, EasyCalc is completely free on both iPhone and Apple Watch. There are zero ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions — just a clean utility we built because we love simple tools.
How does the history tap‑to‑load work?
Open the “History” screen. You’ll see a list of all past equations with results. Tap any entry — the calculator screen will immediately display that expression, and you can edit numbers, change operators, or recalculate. Works like a charm for repeated tasks.
Does EasyCalc support percentage and discount calculations?
Yes! Use the % button for quick percentages. For discounts: enter original price, press “−”, enter discount percentage, then press “%” and “=” to get the final price. Or use dedicated discount mode in the tip/split section. Super intuitive.
Can I use memory functions (M+, M-, MR, MC)?
Absolutely. Store any displayed number into memory with M+, add to memory with M+, recall with MR, and clear memory with MC. Perfect for complex running totals, invoices, or expense tracking.
Why do I need EasyCalc when iPhone has a built-in calculator?
Our app is optimized for one‑handed use, includes a tip & splitter, full history with tap‑to‑reuse, and works seamlessly on Apple Watch with complications. Plus, a clean ad‑free interface with memory buttons always visible.
What about iCloud sync? Do you see my history?
Optional iCloud sync uses Apple’s private CloudKit. Only you can access your data across devices. MingMing Team has no ability to read your history. Sync can be turned off anytime in settings.

🛠️ Quick troubleshooting

  • History not showing? Ensure you’ve performed calculations after installing. History is saved automatically. Restart the app if needed.
  • Apple Watch complication missing? After installing, go to Watch app → Face Gallery → add EasyCalc complication to your active watch face.
  • Buttons feel unresponsive? Force-quit the app and reopen, or restart your watch/iPhone. The app is extremely lightweight but clean install may help.
  • Split bill seems off? Check that you entered number of people correctly. Split calculates total divided equally (including tip if applied).
  • Need to clear memory? Tap “MC” to reset memory register.
